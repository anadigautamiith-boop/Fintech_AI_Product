# Evidence behind the problem statement

Each point gives the figure, its source and its date. Survey results are stated as the sources report them. Figures were checked on 25 November 2026. Where the source could not be read in full, that is said.

## What supports the problem

**People ask for these controls.** In a US survey by Radial and Dynata (1,000 consumers per wave, December 2025 and January 2026), 53% wanted approval before any purchase, 41% wanted two-factor authentication on every transaction and 39% wanted to review or cancel a purchase without penalty ([Chain Store Age](https://chainstoreage.com/consumer-use-agentic-ai-shopping-assistants-lags-interest)). In Accenture's January 2026 survey of 25,590 consumers in 16 countries, including India, 32% would let an agent make the final decision within set limits and 9% would let it shop and buy autonomously ([Chain Store Age](https://chainstoreage.com/accenture-customers-trust-ai-agents-perform-these-tasks-them)).

**Interest runs far ahead of use.** In the same Radial survey, 58% were open to ordering through an AI assistant, 6% had done so, and 5% start shopping with AI tools against 34% who start with search engines. Forrester (9 April 2026) calls adoption low and flat and reports OpenAI discontinuing Instant Checkout ([Forrester](https://www.forrester.com/blogs/agentic-payments-in-b2c-commerce-where-we-are-now)).

**The rail offers one control.** A UPI Reserve Pay block sets one rupee limit per merchant, up to ₹10,000 for up to 90 days. Pine Labs' documentation says revocation goes through the merchant, that many banks allow only one active block per customer, and lists only ICICI and Axis savings accounts ([Pine Labs](https://www.pinelabs.com/docs/online-payments/upi-reserve-pay)). Razorpay describes instant revocation ([Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/technology/news/story/razorpay-npci-launch-agentic-payments-on-anthropics-claude-zomato-swiggy-zepto-go-live-517307-2026-02-20)). Rules such as groceries only or nothing on weekends would have to be enforced above the rail.

**Small, repeat purchases are the natural start.** UPI handled 25.47 billion transactions in October 2026 ([Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/business/upi-transactions-hit-2547-billion-in-october-value-soars-to-3153-lakh-crore), citing NPCI data), and payments up to ₹2,000 are over 95% of UPI merchant volume ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/?p=3164158), 15 September 2026). A retail executive quoted by Retail Brew (14 May 2026) says agents suit simple, repeat, low-risk purchases ([Retail Brew](https://www.retailbrew.com/stories/why-agentic-shopping-adoption-remains-low-despite-the-hype)). The live pilots sit in the same categories: BigBasket and Vi on ChatGPT from October 2025, in private beta ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/09/india-pilots-ai-chatbot-led-e-commerce-with-chatgpt-gemini-claude-in-the-mix/?sidebar=a)), and Zomato, Swiggy and Zepto on Claude from February 2026, with a limited user base (Business Today, linked above).

**Regulators are asking the same questions.** On 29 May 2026 NPCI's chief, Dilip Asbe, asked how limits should be set, how instructions to an agent should be recorded and whether a human approves each payment ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/finance/news/npci-chief-dilip-asbe-bats-for-regulatory-framework-for-fintech-ai-systems-126052901413_1.html)). The RBI's FREE-AI report (13 August 2025) keeps accountability with the institution that deploys an AI system and flags prompt injection and agent collusion as risks ([RBI](https://rbi.org.in/scripts/PublicationReportDetails.aspx?ID=1306)). The RBI's authentication directions, in force since 1 April 2026, require two-factor authentication with a dynamic factor and allow extra risk-based checks ([Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/personal-finance/news/story/rbi-issues-new-guidelines-on-digital-payment-authentication-effective-april-2026-495758-2025-09-25)).

**Proof of intent and liability are unsettled.** Worldpay (30 July 2026) says no liability shift exists for agent errors that fall short of fraud ([Worldpay](https://worldpay.com/insights/articles/agentic-commerce-liability-is-still-being-written)). The Cloud Security Alliance (15 November 2026) notes that on ChatGPT Wallet, launched in the US on 13 November 2026, the instruction, the agent's reasoning and the confirmation screen sit with the agent platform rather than the issuer or merchant, and calls delegated purchases within preset limits unresolved ([CSA](https://labs.cloudsecurityalliance.org/research/csa-research-note-agent-payment-wallet-liability-concentrati/)). In India, Reuters reported on 1 September 2026 that NPCI is preparing a framework to let AI agents make small UPI payments without approval; limits and liability terms have not been published ([Deccan Chronicle](https://www.deccanchronicle.com/technology/india-preparing-framework-to-let-ai-agents-make-small-upi-payments-without-approval-1983986)).

**Agents can be steered.** In Microsoft's Magentic Marketplace simulation, with 100 customer agents and 300 businesses, fake awards, fake reviews and prompt injection moved payments, and most models took the first good-enough option ([The Register](https://www.theregister.com/2025/11/06/microsoft_unveils_the_magentic_marketplace), November 2025). The markets were static, so real behaviour may differ. This is why the policy engine sits outside the model.

**Trust in UPI is already strained.** Reported UPI fraud was 1.34 million cases worth ₹1,087 crore in FY24 ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/economy/news/indians-see-wallets-drained-by-rs-485-cr-in-upi-scams-124112601339_1.html), 26 November 2024) and 10.64 lakh incidents worth ₹805 crore in FY26 through November 2025 ([Madhyamam](https://madhyamamonline.com/india/upi-linked-frauds-amount-to-rs-805-crore-far-fy26-govt-1477281), 15 December 2025). A LocalCircles survey of 32,000+ responses from 365 districts found one in five families with a UPI user had been hit by fraud in three years, and 51% of victims filed no complaint ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/finance/news/upi-transaction-fraud-india-survey-one-in-five-users-hit-localcircles-125062601141_1.html), 26 June 2025).

**Falling model costs hold for a fixed level of capability.** Epoch AI estimates the price of reaching a fixed performance level has fallen about 13x a year since 2023, and the rate slows to about 4.7x a year two years after a level first becomes state of the art ([Epoch AI](https://epoch.ai/publications/the-plunging-price-of-thought), 22 September 2026). A separate paper reports frontier-model prices rising 3x to 18x a year ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.23455v2)). Cost per completed purchase needs its own test, so Reins reports cost per decision.

## What the podcast supports

The idea behind this project came from a Nikhil Kamath podcast episode featuring Razorpay's Harshil Mathur. The only coverage available is a sponsored release dated 31 October 2026 ([Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/spotlight/pr-wire/nikhil-kamath-sits-down-with-the-people-behind-your-payments-network-to-ask-who-really-pays-for-upi-and-what-comes-next), [The Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/business/nikhil-kamath-sits-down-with-the-people-behind-your-payments-network-to-ask-who-really-pays-for-upi-and-what-comes-next)), with no transcript. It credits Mathur with two AI points: model costs will keep falling, and agents in users' own languages could serve people who still book travel and insurance through local agents. Cross-border payments come from the founders of PayGlocal and Dodo Payments, wearables from Seven's Vijay Khubchandani, and tipping appears unattributed. PhonePe's Sameer Nigam is not convinced the economics work yet. The MakeMyTrip example has no source, and the word negotiate appears in [Pine Labs' P3P announcement](https://newsable.asianetnews.com/amp/business/pine-labs-launches-p3p-for-frictionless-aidriven-upi-payments-articleshow-99wnia6) and Accenture's survey, not in remarks credited to Mathur.

Mathur's own timeline has moved closer. In October 2025 he said agentic shopping could become a primary way people buy within five years ([Stocktwits](https://stocktwits.com/news-articles/markets/equity/razorpay-ai-payment-commerce-upi-harshil-mathur-global-fintech-festival-19708219/ch6EZ5WR3i8)). An episode listing from 8 July 2026 puts agents taking over most search and checkout work at about two years ([Apple Podcasts](https://podcasts.apple.com/in/podcast/razorpay-ceo-harshil-mathur-on-how-agentic-commerce/id1841604201?i=1000775890700)). On 10 September 2026 he still called it "early days" ([Newsbytes](https://www.newsbytesapp.com/news/business/harshil-mathur-says-razorpay-tech-ready-for-agentic-commerce/tldr)).

## Where the evidence is thin

- **No transcript.** Attributions come from a sponsored press release, not from the episode.
- **Mostly US or multi-country behaviour data.** No India-only adoption figure and no pilot user numbers were found.
- **Rules not published, or not read at source.** NPCI's agent framework is unpublished, and the RBI authentication directions were read through a news report, not the RBI page.
- **Local-agent users.** No demand data was found for people who book travel and insurance through local agents, so that persona is not built.
- **The rail model is an interpretation.** It follows Pine Labs' public documentation. The assumption that a refund does not restore a block is not from any source.
